                From: Marie Dupuis, Data Protection Officer (DPO)
                To: Jean Lefebvre, Chief Information Officer; Claire Martin, Head of Legal; Thomas Durand, Head of Digital Projects
                Date: September 26, 2024, 09:30 AM
                Cc: Nathalie Dupont, Chief Executive Officer

                Dear Colleagues,

                I am writing to address a matter of great importance regarding the use of language models (LLMs), which are increasingly being integrated into our workflows.

                As DPO, I must express my concerns about the data that could potentially be shared when interacting with these tools. Indeed, when posing questions to LLMs, there is a risk that personal or sensitive data may inadvertently be shared or processed by external systems, beyond our control.

                I would like to remind you that we are legally obligated to ensure that information about our clients, partners, or employees is never exposed to third parties without explicit consent or outside the strict regulatory framework (GDPR). It is therefore imperative that we take steps to mitigate the risks associated with the use of these tools.

                I suggest we organize a meeting promptly to establish clear guidelines on what can and cannot be submitted to LLMs, as well as to explore security measures to regulate these practices.

                I remain available for any questions or clarifications.

                Best regards,
                Marie Dupuis
                Data Protection Officer
